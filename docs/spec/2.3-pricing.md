# 2.3 Pricing — spec

Status: agreed 2026-11-11 (all rules, including those first proposed, accepted).
Parent: `docs/plan.md` §2.3. Depends on 2.1 (permissions, audit, outbox), 2.2 (variants, units,
tax classes, scale barcodes) and 2.5 (customers, groups, tiers).

Decided 2026-11-11:
- **Tax mode** is one company setting: prices are tax-**inclusive** by default, and a company may
  switch to tax-exclusive. Every price in the install follows it.
- **Tax rates** have a history per tax class (rate + valid-from date), so a future change can be
  entered ahead of time. A company switch **"not tax-registered"** (non-PKP) turns tax off.
- **Price-list entries** are a **fixed price** or a **rule**: **± N %** or **± Rp amount**.
- A rule is relative to the **next applicable price**, i.e. what the customer would pay without
  this list (Gold −5% at a branch selling at 11 000 → 10 450).
- **No rounding step** for computed prices. Money is whole Rupiah, so results are rounded half up
  to Rp 1 and nothing more.
- **Quantity tiers** price the **whole line** at the tier, not incrementally. They count the
  **same variant across the whole cart** in base units.
- A list with **no matching entry or tier falls through** to the next list.
- **Unit fallback:** a unit with no price in any list is priced at the base-unit price × factor.
- **No price** anywhere → `no_price`. A user with `price.override` may type a price.
- **Validity dates per entry.** A whole list can also be switched off.
- **Price-embedded scale labels:** the printed amount is final. Member prices do not change it.
- **Manual price override:** `price.override` gets a **MaxPct** limit, i.e. how far below the
  resolved price a user may go. Raising a price is always allowed with the permission.
- **Cash rounding** is a company setting, **off** by default.

## 1. Structure

```
PriceList ── kind (default | branch | group | tier | customer), target, active, list rule?
   └── PriceEntry ── variant + unit, valid_from?, valid_to?
          └── PriceTier ── min_qty → fixed price | rule (±% / ±Rp)
TaxClass (2.2) ── TaxRate ── rate (basis points), valid_from
Company settings: tax_inclusive, tax_registered, cash_rounding
```

## 2. Price lists

| Kind | Target | How many |
|---|---|---|
| `default` | – | exactly one, seeded, cannot be archived or switched off |
| `branch` | a branch | at most one active per branch |
| `group` | a customer group (2.5) | at most one active per group |
| `tier` | a membership tier (2.5) | at most one active per tier |
| `customer` | one customer (contract price) | at most one active per customer |

- A list has a `name`, `active` flag and `archived_at`. Switching a list off or archiving it removes it
  from resolution at once. A second active list for the same target → `409 price_list_conflict`.
- A list may have one **list rule** (± N % or ± Rp), which prices every item that has no
  entry in the list, e.g. "Gold: −5% on everything". The `default` list cannot have a rule
  (nothing comes after it) → `422 invalid_rule`.
- % rules are stored in basis points (−5% = −500). A rule may not produce a price ≤ 0. Such a
  result counts as no price from this list.

## 3. Entries and tiers

- An **entry** is (list, variant, unit) with optional `valid_from` / `valid_to` (local dates,
  inclusive, in the selling branch's time zone). Two entries of one list for the same variant +
  unit with overlapping dates → `409 price_overlap`. This is how a price change is scheduled:
  the old entry ends on 30 Nov and the new one starts on 1 Dec.
- An entry has **1–10 tiers**: `min_qty` (> 0, in the entry's unit, 3 dp) → a fixed price
  (minor units, ≥ 0; 0 is allowed for free items) or a rule. Tier `min_qty` values are unique within
  an entry. The first tier need not start at 1 (§4, fall through).
- A rule in an entry overrides the list rule for that item.
- Entries reference a variant + unit, never a product (2.2). Archived variants keep their entries.

## 4. Resolution (in `core`, pure)

Input: the cart lines (variant, unit, qty), the branch, the sale date (branch time zone) and the
customer (optional). Output per line: the unit price, the list and tier it came from, and the
line amount.

1. **The chain** for this sale, in order, skipping absent, inactive or archived lists:
   contract list of the customer → tier list (only when the tier applies on the date, 2.5 §4) →
   group list → branch list → default list.
2. **Counted quantity** `Q` of a variant = the sum over all cart lines of that variant, in base units
   (10 pcs + 1 pack(6) → 16). A tier's `min_qty` is converted to base units for comparison
   (from 2 box(24) = 48). When the cart changes, all lines of the variant are re-priced.
3. **Price of (variant, unit) from list *i*:**
   - The list has a valid entry for (variant, unit) **and** a tier with the highest
     `min_qty ≤ Q` → that tier's fixed price, or its rule applied to the price from lists *i+1…*.
   - Otherwise, if the list has a **list rule** → the rule applied to the price from lists *i+1…*.
   - Otherwise → the price from lists *i+1…* (fall through).
   - Past the end of the chain → none.
   - A rule whose base is none → none.
4. **Unit fallback:** if no list in the chain has an entry for (variant, unit) with a matching tier
   (rules alone do not count), the unit price is the base-unit price from step 3 × the unit factor.
   Example: pcs 10 000, no box price → 1 box(24) = 240 000. A Gold −5% list rule applies once,
   inside the base-unit price.
5. **No price** → the line is refused with `no_price`, unless the product is `open_price` or a
   price is typed through a manual override (§6).
6. **Rule arithmetic:** `base × (10 000 + bp) / 10 000` and `base ± amount`, rounded half up to Rp 1.
   Example: 10 450 −5% = 9 927.5 → 9 928.
7. **Line amount** = unit price × qty (3 dp), rounded half up to Rp 1
   (1.255 kg × 12 990 = 16 302.45 → 16 302).

## 5. Special lines

- **Open price** (2.2 `open_price`): the cashier always types the unit price. Lists are ignored,
  except that the resolved price, if any, is shown as a suggestion. No permission is needed.
- **Weight-embedded scale label:** qty = weight, priced normally (member prices apply).
- **Price-embedded scale label:** the line amount is the printed amount, **final**. Its qty is derived
  from the price the scale uses: the chain **without** customer lists (branch → default). qty =
  amount ÷ that unit price, rounded to 3 dp (2.2 §6). No price there → qty is left empty and the line
  is still sold at the printed amount. Customer prices and tiers never change it.
- **Bundle (kit):** priced like any variant, from its own entries. Components are not priced.

## 6. Manual price override

- Changes a line's unit price at the till. It needs `price.override`, whose new **MaxPct** limit is
  the most a user may go **below** the resolved price: `(resolved − new) / resolved ≤ limit`.
  Beyond the limit, a manager override (2.1 §6) is needed. **Raising** the price needs only the permission.
- With no resolved price (`no_price`), any price ≥ 0 may be typed with `price.override`, and the
  limit does not apply.
- Every override is audited: line, resolved price, new price, user, approver. A later change to the
  cart that would re-price the line keeps the overridden price.
- Seed: Manager `price.override` 20%. Cashier has no `price.override`. Existing installs keep the
  grants they have (no limit = unlimited).

## 7. Tax

- **Rates:** `TaxRate` (tax class, rate in basis points, `valid_from` local date). The rate on a sale
  is the one with the latest `valid_from ≤ sale date`. Rates can be added for future dates. A
  rate already used by a posted sale is never edited or deleted.
- Seed: `Standard` 11% (1 100 bp) from 2022-04-01, and `Exempt` 0%. Extra classes (e.g. PPnBM goods)
  are added at HQ.
- **Tax-inclusive** (default): the price includes PPN. Line tax = `amount × rate / (10 000 + rate)`,
  rounded half up to Rp 1. Net = amount − tax.
- **Tax-exclusive:** line tax = `amount × rate / 10 000`, rounded half up to Rp 1, and added on top.
- Tax is computed **per line** and summed. Cart-level discounts (2.4) and their tax effect are
  settled with 2.4.
- **Not tax-registered:** no tax is computed or printed, and the tax report is empty. Prices are
  charged as entered.
- Settings `tax_inclusive` and `tax_registered` are HQ-only and audited. Changing `tax_inclusive`
  does **not** convert stored prices. The back office warns before saving.
  Default `tax_registered`: on.

## 8. Cash rounding

- Company setting `cash_rounding`: `none` (default), `nearest_100`, `down_100`, `nearest_500`,
  `down_500`.
- It applies only when cash settles the rest of the sale. The rounded difference is stored on the
  sale as `cash_rounding` (+/−) and shown in reports and the Z report. Card, QRIS and other tenders
  pay the exact amount. Built with 2.6.

## 9. Permissions, sync, events

- New permission `price.manage` (HQ only): price lists, entries, tax rates and the price settings.
  Seed: Owner, Manager.
- `price.override` gets a limit kind `MaxPct` (2.1 §3 table updated).
- Price lists, entries and tax rates are HQ master data (`403 read_only_replica` on branch nodes),
  audited (entry old → new prices) and written to the outbox in the same transaction.
- WebSocket event `price.changed {variant_ids}` (`Audience::Everyone`). Registers re-price open
  carts and refresh the shown item.

## 12. Test cases

`core` (table-driven):
1. Default only: 10 000 → 10 000; qty 15 with tier 9 000 from 12 → 15 × 9 000 = 135 000.
2. Tiers count the whole cart: 10 pcs + 1 pack(6) → Q = 16 → both lines at the tier; remove the
   pack → back to 10 000.
3. Chain order: contract beats tier beats group beats branch beats default.
4. Fall through: Wholesale has only "8 500 from 12"; 5 pcs → default 10 000; 12 pcs → 8 500.
5. Rule on next price: Bali branch 11 000, Gold list rule −5% → 10 450; outside Bali → 9 500.
6. Rule ± Rp: Wholesale −500 on 10 000 → 9 500; rule result ≤ 0 → no price from that list.
7. Rule arithmetic rounding: 10 450 −5% → 9 928; 333 −10% → 300 (299.7 → 300).
8. Entry rule overrides list rule; list rule applies to items without an entry.
9. Unit fallback: box(24) without price → 24 × pcs price; box with a price in the branch list → that
   price; Gold −5% list rule + no box price → 24 × (pcs price −5%).
10. No price anywhere → `no_price`; open price → typed price; rule with no base → `no_price`.
11. Validity: entry ends 30 Nov, new entry from 1 Dec; a sale at 23:30 WIB on 30 Nov uses the old
    one. Overlapping dates → `price_overlap`.
12. Tier applies only when valid on the date (2.5 §4): expired Gold → falls to the next list.
13. Manual override: Manager 20%: 10 000 → 8 000 ok, 7 999 → manager override needed; 12 000 ok.
14. Tax inclusive 11%: 11 100 → tax 1 100, net 10 000; 10 000 → tax 991 (990.99); exempt → 0.
15. Tax exclusive 11%: 10 000 → tax 1 100, total 11 100. Not registered → no tax.
16. Tax rate history: 11% until 2024-12-31, 12% from 2025-01-01 → the sale date picks the rate.
17. Line amount: 1.255 kg × 12 990 → 16 302.
18. Price-embedded label 15 000 with Gold customer → 15 000; qty from branch/default price.
19. Cash rounding: 47 830 → nearest_100 47 800, down_500 47 500, none 47 830; card → no rounding.

Server (HTTP, SQLite + MySQL):
20. `PUT /price-lists/{id}` create → replay → one list; second active branch list → `price_list_conflict`.
21. Entry with overlapping dates → `409 price_overlap`; default list rule → `422 invalid_rule`.
22. Branch node → `read_only_replica`; user without `price.manage` → `permission_denied`.
23. `POST /pricing/quote` with a Gold customer at a branch → the prices from cases 3–9.
24. Every write: one audit row and one outbox row in the same transaction; `price.changed` sent.

## 15. Clarifications (from R1, 2026-11-11)

1. A rule (list or entry) whose result is ≤ 0, or which has no base, prices nothing from that list:
   the price from the lists after it applies. An entry rule that fails does not fall back to the
   list's own rule.
2. Unit fallback (§4.4) is skipped as soon as any list has a matching entry tier for the unit, even
   if that tier is a rule without a base (the line is then `no_price`).
3. Price-embedded label lines do not count toward other lines' quantity tiers, and their store unit
   price is the price for one unit (no tiers).
4. The default list may hold no rules at all: a rule inside one of its entries is `invalid_rule`, a
   % rule of −100% or less is `invalid_rule`, and switching the default list off or archiving it is
   `invalid_field`.
5. Validation errors without a named code (tier count, `min_qty` ≤ 0 or repeated, negative price,
   `valid_from` after `valid_to`) use `invalid_field`. An open-price line without a typed price is
   `no_price` with the resolved price as a suggestion.
6. Negative amounts (returns) round half away from zero. Cash rounding "down" rounds toward zero.

From R2 (`db`, 2026-11-11):

7. A list's kind and target cannot change after it is created (`invalid_field`). Only the seeded
   default list exists; no other `default` list can be created.
8. Tax rates are keyed by (class, `valid_from`). Writing a rate for an existing day changes it.
   There is no delete. `Exempt` 0% is seeded from 2000-01-01.
9. An entry's unit must belong to the variant's product (`invalid_reference`). An entry id already
   used in another list → `invalid_field`.
10. Entries are listed in pages by entry id (at most 1 000 per page).

From R3 (`server`, 2026-11-11):

11. `price.changed` carries `{scope, variant_ids}`; `variant_ids` is `null` for list, tax-rate and
    settings changes (any price may have changed).
12. The quote is a read: no `Idempotency-Key`, and it does not check `price.override` or its limit
    (that belongs to 2.6). Quantities must be > 0 (returns come with 2.6), at most 500 lines. An
    unknown variant or a unit of another product is a per-line `invalid_reference`; an unknown,
    archived, merged or erased customer rejects the whole quote (`422 invalid_reference`).
13. A tax class without a rate on the date counts as 0%. Not tax-registered → `rate_bp` 0.
14. The lookup price of a unit counts that unit as its base quantity, so quantity tiers apply to the
    box lookup price (1 box(24) at "from 12 pcs 9 000" → 216 000).
15. Price-list, entry, rate and settings reads need a user session; only the quote and the lookup
    accept a device token alone. `price.manage` counts from any role assignment, like
    `catalog.manage`.
16. `GET /products/{id}/prices` returns every non-archived list and all the product's entries,
    including entries in archived lists.
