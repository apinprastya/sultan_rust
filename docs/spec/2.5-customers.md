# 2.5 Customers & membership — spec

Status: agreed 2026-11-11 (all rules, including those first proposed, accepted).
Parent: `docs/plan.md` §2.5. Depends on 2.1 (branches, registers, permissions, audit, outbox).
Spec 2.3 Pricing is settled after this one and links price lists to customers, groups and tiers.

Decided 2026-11-11:
- **Scope: the P1 part only.** Customer profile, customer groups, membership tiers, lookup at the
  till, purchase history, the hooks 2.3 needs for price lists, and the customer balance for saved
  change (§11). Receivables and credit limits go with 2.9 Money. Points, store credit, and deposit
  top-up and withdrawal get their own spec in P2.
- **Identity:** every customer has an auto **code**, the key printed on the member card or QR.
  **Phone** is optional, but **unique** when set, and is the fast lookup at the till.
- **Code format:** a **random short code** such as `K7QH-29XM`, created anywhere without
  coordinating with HQ.
- **Sign-up at the till and in the back office.** A cashier with a permission can sign up a
  customer at the register, also while HQ is offline. The record syncs to HQ.
- **Editing:** the till may fix the profile (name, phone, email, address) **only while HQ is
  reachable**. Group, tier, merge, archive and erase are **HQ back office only**.
- **Groups and tiers:** a customer has **at most one** customer group and **at most one**
  membership tier.
- **Tiers are assigned by hand** for now, with an **optional end date**. Automatic upgrade and
  downgrade by rolling spend is specified in §5 but built after sync brings all sales to HQ.
- **Fields** besides code, name, phone, email, birthday and address: **company name + tax ID
  (NPWP/NIK) + tax address**, and **notes**.
- **Personal data** (UU 27/2022 PDP): delete = **archive**. A separate, audited **erase** action
  blanks the personal data but keeps the record and its sales. Old audit entries are not
  scrubbed (§7).
- **Lookup at the till:** by exact phone or by member code (typed or scanned). **Name search needs a
  permission.**
- **Purchase history:** the full history is visible in the back office. The till shows recent receipts.
- **Scope:** customers belong to the **whole company**. The branch where a customer signed up is recorded.
- **Duplicates** are **merged at HQ**.
- **Receipt** shows the name and a masked phone, e.g. `Budi S. · 0812****789 · Gold`.
- **Customer balance (saved change, "simpan kembalian")**, added 2026-11-11 (§11): when coins for
  change are short, the change can be saved to the customer's balance and used as payment later.
  In P1 the balance only **grows from saved change** and **shrinks when it pays**. Top-up and
  withdrawal come with the P2 deposit spec. Limits are company settings (default **Rp 5 000 per
  sale**, **Rp 100 000 per customer**). A branch spends the balance it knows, even offline. The
  balance **never expires**, and an erase is refused while the balance is not zero.

## 1. Structure

```
CustomerGroup (Wholesale, Reseller, …)      MembershipTier (Silver, Gold, Platinum; ranked)
        ▲ 0..1                                       ▲ 0..1 (+ valid_until)
        └──────────────── Customer ──────────────────┘
                          code, name, phone, …, signed_up_branch
                          merged_into (0..1 → Customer)
```

Price lists (2.3) attach to a group, a tier, or one customer (contract price). This spec only
reserves those links. 2.3 defines the lists.

## 2. Customer

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `id` | UUIDv7, created by the client (2.1). |
| `code` | 8 characters from the alphabet `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ` (no `0 O 1 I L U`), random, created by whoever creates the customer. Stored without the dash (`K7QH29XM`), shown as `K7QH-29XM`. Input is upper-cased and the dash is optional. Unique. Never changes. |
| `name` | Required, 1–100 characters after trimming. |
| `phone` | Optional. Normalized to `+62…` digits: `0812-3456-789`, `62812 3456 789` and `+62 812 3456 789` all become `+628123456789`. Other country codes are kept as typed (digits after `+`). 8–15 digits after normalization, otherwise `invalid_phone`. **Unique** among non-archived customers. |
| `email` | Optional, contains one `@`, stored lower case. Not unique. |
| `birthday` | Optional month + day, with an optional year (stored as `birth_month`, `birth_day`, `birth_year` nullable). 29 February is allowed. |
| `address` | Optional free text. |
| `company_name`, `tax_id`, `tax_address` | Optional. `tax_id` is 15 or 16 digits (NPWP or NIK) when set, otherwise `invalid_tax_id`. |
| `notes` | Optional free text for staff. Not printed. |
| `group_id` | 0..1 customer group (§3). |
| `tier_id`, `tier_valid_until` | 0..1 membership tier (§4) and an optional last valid date. |
| `signed_up_branch_id`, `signed_up_at` | Set when the customer is created. Never change. |
| `archived_at`, `merged_into_id`, `erased_at` | See §7 and §8. |

- The sale does not depend on any of these fields except the price links. A posted sale stores the
  `customer_id` and a **snapshot** of the receipt line (name, masked phone, tier name), so later
  edits or an erase never change an old receipt.
- **Walk-in sales** have no customer. A customer is optional on every sale in P1. A credit sale (2.9)
  will require one.

### Code clashes

8 characters from 30 symbols gives 30⁸ ≈ 6.6 × 10¹¹ codes. The database still has a unique index:
- A local insert that hits the index draws a new code and tries again (up to 5 times, then `500`).
- A clash at HQ during sync (two branches drew the same code offline) is practically impossible.
  If it happens, the later record keeps its id and gets a new code, and the change syncs back.
  The old card then no longer finds it, so the clash is shown in the back office as
  "code changed".

## 3. Customer groups

- `CustomerGroup`: `id`, `name` (unique, case-sensitive like all text), `archived_at`.
  2.3 links a price list to it.
- Used for wholesale/reseller pricing. No seeded groups.
- Archiving a group that customers still use is allowed. They keep the link, but the group's price
  list stops applying. The back office shows those customers so they can be moved.

## 4. Membership tiers

- `MembershipTier`: `id`, `name` (unique), `rank` (integer, higher = better, unique), `archived_at`.
  2.3 links a price list to it. P2 adds the points earning rate and benefits.
- No seeded tiers (the setup wizard may offer Silver/Gold/Platinum later).
- A customer's tier **applies on a sale date** when `tier_id` is set, the tier is not archived and
  `tier_valid_until` is empty or ≥ the sale's local date in the selling branch's time zone.
  On `2026-12-31` a tier valid until `2026-12-31` still applies; on `2027-01-01` it does not.
- The till and the back office show an expired tier as "Gold (expired 2026-12-31)". Renewal sets a
  new `tier_valid_until` in the back office.
- Every tier change is audited: old tier/date → new tier/date and who made it.

## 5. Automatic tiers (specified now, built after HQ sync)

- Per tier, an optional **qualifying spend**: minimum net sales (after returns) over the last
  **12 months**, across all branches.
- A daily job at HQ moves a customer to the **highest** tier whose threshold they meet. It
  **downgrades** only when `tier_valid_until` has passed, so a paid or manual tier is never cut short.
- A customer marked **"manual tier"** is never changed by the job.
- Each change is audited as `system` and synced like a manual change.

## 6. Lookup at the till

- **By phone:** an exact match after normalization. Returns at most one customer.
- **By code:** typed (dash optional) or scanned from the member card (Code 128 of `K7QH-29XM`) or QR.
  Customer lookup is its own step on the register (a key and button, defined with 2.6), so a member
  code is never confused with a product barcode in the scan field.
- **By name:** a substring search over name and company name, at most 20 results. Needs
  `customer.search`.
- Lookup needs a **user session** (a cashier is logged in). A device token alone sees no customers.
- An archived customer is not found. A merged customer's code and phone find the customer it was
  merged into (§8). An erased customer is not found.
- The result shows the name, masked phone, group, tier (with an expiry warning) and the 5 most
  recent receipts (§9).

## 7. Sign-up, edit, archive, erase

| Action | Where | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up (create) | Till (works offline) and back office | `customer.create` |
| Edit profile (name, phone, email, birthday, address, company, tax id, notes) | Back office at HQ; till only while HQ is reachable | `customer.edit` |
| Set group, tier, valid until, manual-tier flag | Back office at HQ | `customer.manage` |
| Archive / unarchive | Back office at HQ | `customer.manage` |
| Merge (§8) | Back office at HQ | `customer.manage` |
| Erase personal data | Back office at HQ | `customer.erase` |
| Manage groups and tiers | Back office at HQ | `customer.manage` |

- **Sign-up** at the till asks for the name and phone (optional) and may fill the other profile
  fields. The till cannot set a group or tier.
- A phone already used by another non-archived customer at this node → `409 phone_taken` with
  that customer's code and masked phone, so the cashier can pick them instead.
- **Offline sign-up at a branch:** the branch checks the phone only against what it knows. At HQ, a
  second customer with the same phone is **kept** but its phone is moved to `phone_conflict`,
  `phone` is cleared and it appears in the back office's **"Possible duplicates"** list for a merge.
- **Editing at the till while HQ is unreachable** → `503 hq_unreachable`. On a standalone or
  single-app install, HQ is local, so this never happens.
- **Archive** hides the customer from lookup and sign-up checks. Sales stay linked. Unarchive is
  refused with `409 phone_taken` if another customer took the phone meanwhile.
- **Erase** (personal-data request) blanks name (→ `Erased customer`), phone, email, birthday,
  address, company name, tax id, tax address and notes, archives the record and sets `erased_at`.
  It **cannot be undone**. Sales, audit entries and receipt snapshots stay. The audit entry records
  that an erase happened and by whom, but **not the erased values**.
- **Decided 2026-11-11:** older audit entries keep their full before/after values and are **not
  scrubbed**, because `audit_log` stays append-only. Known limitation: after an erase, the
  personal data still exists in the audit log (readable with `audit.view`) and in already-synced
  outbox payloads.

## 8. Merge

`POST /customers/{a}/merge` with `{from: b}` (needs `Idempotency-Key`):
- B must not be archived, erased or merged already, and A ≠ B.
- A keeps its own fields. Empty fields of A are filled from B (phone included if A has none).
  A keeps its own group and tier. If A has no tier, it takes B's, and the same for the group.
- B is archived with `merged_into_id = A`. B's **code** and B's **phone** (if it did not move to
  A) still find A at the till, by following `merged_into_id`.
- Posted documents are **not edited**. A's history (§9) includes B's documents.
- Merging into a customer that was itself merged → `409 merged`. Chains are never created.
- Audited, with one outbox event for each of A and B.

## 9. Purchase history

- **Back office:** all of the customer's posted sales and returns across branches (with a merged
  customer's documents included), newest first, paged. Built with 2.6 when sales exist.
- **Till:** the 5 most recent receipts (last 90 days) with date, branch, number and total. On a
  branch node they come from the branch's own data, so receipts from other branches appear only
  after sync. Used to start a return by receipt (2.6).

## 10. Receipt

- With a customer: `Budi S. · 0812****789 · Gold` (one line).
  - **Name:** the first word in full, then the initials of the other words (`Budi Santoso Wijaya`
    → `Budi S. W.`). A company customer prints the company name in full.
  - **Phone:** the first 4 and last 3 digits of the local form are kept and the rest masked
    (`+628123456789` → `0812****789`). No phone → omitted.
  - **Tier:** the tier name when it applies on the sale date. Otherwise omitted.
- When `tax_id` is set and the sale is a wholesale sale (2.6), the company name, tax id and tax address are
  printed in full (needed for a tax invoice).

## 11. Customer balance (saved change)

An append-only **balance ledger** per customer (like the stock and payment ledgers, `docs/plan.md`
§1). The balance is the sum of its entries, cached on the customer and never edited directly.

| Entry kind | Amount | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `change_saved` | + | a cash sale whose change is (partly) saved (2.6) |
| `payment` | − | the balance used as a tender (2.6) |
| `change_saved_reversed` / `payment_reversed` | − / + | voiding or refunding that sale (2.6), so the balance goes back exactly |
| `payout` | − | paying the balance out in cash before an erase (below) |
| `merge_out` / `merge_in` | − / + | a merge (§8) moves B's balance to A |

Each entry has `id` (UUIDv7), `customer_id`, `kind`, `amount`, `branch_id`, `register_id`,
`user_id`, `cashier_session_id` (till entries), `document_id` (sale or payout) and `occurred_at`.

**Saving change** (at the till, on a cash sale with a customer attached):
- The cashier enters the amount to save, from Rp 1 up to the change due. It can be partial: the
  change is 3 700, the cashier gives 3 000 and saves 700.
- Limits (company settings, HQ only, audited): `max_saved_per_sale` (default Rp 5 000) and
  `max_balance` (default Rp 100 000). Saving more than either → `422 balance_limit`, and the error
  shows the most that can still be saved. `max_saved_per_sale = 0` turns the feature off.
- The saved amount stays in the drawer: expected cash in the cashier session = cash received −
  cash change actually given. The close report shows "change saved to balance" as its own line.
- Needs a customer. A walk-in customer can be signed up on the spot (§7), so no extra permission
  is needed beyond `sale.create`.
- The receipt prints `Saved to balance: Rp 700 · Balance: Rp 2 450`.

**Paying with the balance:**
- The balance is a tender (2.6) for any amount up to the balance. It can be part of a split payment.
- It needs the customer attached. The till shows the balance in the customer lookup (§6).
- Paying more than the balance → `422 insufficient_balance`.
- **Offline (branch node):** the branch checks the balance it knows, i.e. HQ's last synced balance
  plus this branch's own entries since then. If sync later shows a **negative** balance (spent at
  two branches while offline), HQ keeps it, flags the customer under "Negative balances" in the back
  office, and the next saved change fills it. On a standalone install or single app the balance is
  always exact.

**Archive, merge, erase:**
- Archive keeps the balance. It cannot be used until the customer is unarchived.
- Merge moves B's balance to A in the same transaction (`merge_out` on B, `merge_in` on A).
  The result may exceed `max_balance`, and that is allowed.
- **Erase is refused while the balance is not 0** → `409 balance_not_zero`. The customer first
  spends it, or a manager **pays it out** at the till: a cash-out from the drawer in the current
  cashier session with reason "balance payout", a `payout` entry, permission
  `customer.balance_payout`, and a printed slip. A negative balance must be settled (saved change
  or written off by a balance payout with a negative amount, Owner only) before an erase.
- No expiry.

**Reports:** total outstanding balance (money owed to customers, a liability) per branch and
company, and the balance movements per customer (back office). Both are built with 2.10.

## 12. Permissions, sync, events

New permissions (`core::access::Permission`):

| Key | Meaning | Seed |
|---|---|---|
| `customer.create` | sign up customers | Owner, Manager, Cashier |
| `customer.search` | search customers by name at the till | Owner, Manager |
| `customer.edit` | edit the profile | Owner, Manager |
| `customer.manage` | groups, tiers, archive, merge (HQ only) | Owner, Manager |
| `customer.erase` | erase personal data (HQ only) | Owner |
| `customer.balance_payout` | pay a customer balance out in cash at the till (§11) | Owner, Manager |

- Lookup by phone or code needs any user session (§6).
- **Sync:** customers, groups and tiers are company-wide and replicated to every branch, so lookup
  works offline. A customer **created at a branch** flows up to HQ in the branch outbox and then back
  down to the other branches. All other customer writes happen at HQ (`403 read_only_replica`
  on a branch node for anything except sign-up).
- Writes are audited and add an outbox row in the same transaction (2.1).
- **Balance entries** are transactional data like sales: a branch creates them, and they flow up
  to HQ in the branch outbox. HQ sends the updated balance back down with the customer.
- WebSocket event `customer.changed {customer_id}` (`Audience::Everyone`, no personal data), so an
  open till refreshes the attached customer.

## 15. Test cases

`core` (table-driven):
1. Phone normalization: `0812-3456-789`, `62812 3456 789`, `+62 812 3456 789` → `+628123456789`;
   `+65 9123 4567` → `+6591234567`; 7 digits / letters → `invalid_phone`.
2. Code: generated codes use only the alphabet and are 8 long; `k7qh-29xm` and `K7QH29XM` parse to
   the same code; `K7QH-29X0` (0 not in the alphabet) → not a code.
3. Tax id: 15 and 16 digits ok, 14 or letters → `invalid_tax_id`.
4. Tier applies: no valid-until → yes; valid-until = sale date → yes; the day after → no; archived
   tier → no. The sale date uses the branch time zone (23:30 WIB on the 31st is still the 31st).
5. Receipt mask: `Budi Santoso Wijaya` → `Budi S. W.`; `Budi` → `Budi`; phone `+628123456789` →
   `0812****789`; foreign phone `+6591234567` → `+659***567`.
6. Merge field rules: A's fields win; empty A fields are filled from B; A without tier takes B's tier.
7. Balance: entries +700, +2 000, −500 → 2 200; save 6 000 with a 5 000 per-sale limit →
   `balance_limit` (max 5 000); save 3 000 with balance 98 000 and max 100 000 → `balance_limit`
   (max 2 000); pay 2 300 with 2 200 → `insufficient_balance`; void the paying sale → +500 back.
8. Saved change in the drawer: cash 50 000 for a 46 300 sale, give 3 000, save 700 → expected cash
   +47 000, balance +700.
9. Auto tier (§5): spend 12 months 5 M with thresholds Silver 1 M / Gold 5 M → Gold; spend drops
   but valid-until not passed → unchanged; manual flag → unchanged.

Server (HTTP, SQLite + MySQL):
10. `PUT /customers/{id}` create → replay → one customer; a cashier creates, a cashier update → `permission_denied`.
11. Duplicate phone → `409 phone_taken` with the other customer's code; archived holder → allowed.
12. Lookup by phone and code; by merged customer's code → finds A; archived and erased → `404`.
13. Name search without `customer.search` → `permission_denied`; device token only → `401`.
14. A cashier changing the tier through `PUT` → `permission_denied` (needs `customer.manage`).
15. Branch node: sign-up ok, edit/archive/merge/erase → `read_only_replica` (till edit while HQ is
    online is forwarded, built with sync).
16. Erase: fields blanked, record archived, sale still linked, one audit entry for the erase (older
    entries untouched), second erase → `404`/no-op; balance ≠ 0 → `409 balance_not_zero`.
17. Merge: B archived with `merged_into`, merge into a merged customer → `409 merged`, A = B → `422`;
    B's balance moves to A (`merge_out` + `merge_in` in one transaction).
18. Balance payout: without `customer.balance_payout` → `permission_denied`; without an open cashier
    session → rejected; payout 2 200 → balance 0 and the session's expected cash −2 200; replay with
    the same `Idempotency-Key` → one payout.
19. Balance settings: writes need `customer.manage`; a negative limit → `422`.
20. Every write: one audit row and one outbox row in the same transaction; `customer.changed` is sent.

## 18. Clarifications (from M2, 2026-11-11)

1. An archived customer cannot be edited: `PUT` → `archived` (`merged` if it was merged). Unarchive
   first.
2. Merging a customer into itself → `422 invalid_field`. Duplicate group or tier names or ranks →
   `409 conflict`. A code clash on create → `conflict`; the caller draws a new code and retries.
3. `phone_conflict` is cleared only by a merge or an erase. Archived customers leave the
   "Possible duplicates" list. The "Negative balances" list includes archived customers.
4. Erase also works on an archived customer. An erased customer that was merged keeps
   `merged_into`, so its old code still finds the customer it was merged into.
5. A merge re-points customers merged into B earlier to A, so lookups never follow a chain. Its two
   balance entries ride on the two `customer.put` outbox rows, which carry the new balances.
6. `customer.balance_payout` is not overridable: a manager pays out under their own login.
7. `tier_valid_until` is stored as a `YYYYMMDD` integer, like `cashier_session.local_day`.

From M1 (`core::customer`, 2026-11-11):

8. Phone input: the separators ` -.()` are dropped. Digits without `+` are Indonesian (`0…` → `62…`,
   `62…` kept, anything else gets `62` in front). With `+`, the number may not start with `0`.
   `00` is not read as an international prefix.
9. Phone mask: keep the first 4 characters of the local form and its last 3 digits, with one `*`
   per hidden digit (`0812****789`, `+659***567`).
10. Tax id: the separators ` .-` are dropped, so `01.234.567.8-901.000` is accepted.
11. Saving change: every limit returns `balance_limit` with `max` = min(change due, per-sale limit,
    `max_balance` − balance), never below 0. Saving 0 or less is `invalid_field`.
12. A payout never moves the balance past 0 (0 < amount ≤ balance, or balance ≤ amount < 0);
    otherwise `insufficient_balance`.
13. Merge checks, in order: A = B → `invalid_field`; A or B already merged → `merged`; B archived or
    erased → `archived`. A keeps its own manual-tier flag.
14. Auto tier: archived tiers and tiers without a threshold are never chosen; a tier without
    `valid_until` is never downgraded.
