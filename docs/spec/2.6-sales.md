# 2.6 Sales (register) — spec

Status: agreed 2026-11-16 (decisions D1–D14 below).
Parent: `docs/plan.md` §2.6. Depends on 2.1 (permissions, overrides, cashier sessions, audit,
outbox), 2.2 (variants, units, barcodes, bundles, scale labels), 2.3 (quote, override, tax, cash
rounding) and 2.5 (customers, saved change, customer balance as a tender).

## 0. Decisions (2026-11-16)

- **D1 Cart in progress** lives only in the register's memory and is posted in one request. The
  server checks prices and permissions again at posting.
- **D2 Crash copy:** the register writes the cart to a small local file (next to `register.token`)
  on every change and restores it on the next login for the same cashier session.
- **D3 No selling while the branch server is down** (P1). The branch server keeps selling without
  HQ (plan §1), and a single app is never affected.
- **D4 Tender types are HQ data** with a fixed **kind**: `cash`, `card`, `qris`, `transfer`,
  `ewallet`, `customer_balance`, `other` (e.g. "Debit BCA" is a card, "QRIS BRI" is a qris).
  2.9 later links each tender type to a payment account.
- **D5 QRIS, cards and e-wallets are recorded by hand:** the cashier confirms on the EDC or phone
  and types a reference. Payment-provider integration comes later.
- **D6 No card surcharge.** Bank Indonesia does not allow passing the card fee (MDR) on to the
  customer, so surcharges stay out (later only if a real need appears).
- **D7 Manual discounts:** line and whole-cart discounts now. The cart discount is split over the
  lines (§6), which also settles its tax (the question 2.3 §7 left for 2.4).
- **D8 Same item scanned twice** adds to the existing line, except weighed items, scale labels,
  open-price items and overridden lines, which always get a new line.
- **D9 Stock movements now:** sales and returns write a minimal stock ledger in base units at the
  branch's default location. Negative stock is allowed, and there is no costing yet (2.8).
- **D10 Post-void any time** with `sale.post_void`. The voiding user needs an open cashier session
  on this register; the money goes out of the **current** session's drawer and shows in its
  report. The original session's closed report is never changed.
- **D11 Refunds go to cash or the customer balance only**, for returns and post-voids alike.
  Card, QRIS, transfer and e-wallet payments are paid back in cash or to the balance.
- **D12 Exchange is one flow:** the return's refund pays the new sale, and the customer settles
  the difference.
- **D13 Tax-invoice switch:** a sale with a customer that has a `tax_id` can be marked as a tax
  invoice, which prints the full company block (2.5 §10). Pay later waits for 2.9.
- **D14 Register keys** follow the old Qt till where they fit (§13). Session home: close session
  moves F10 → Ctrl+F10, detach customer F4 → Ctrl+F3, and the F2 "test override" is removed.
- Also decided: **every removed line is a void** (§3); a whole-cart discount does **not** apply to
  a price-embedded scale label (§6); imported old-Sultan sales are **read-only**, so they cannot be
  returned (§14); returns by receipt from another branch once its sale has synced, within a
  `return_days` window (default 30) (§8); held carts are never dropped automatically (§4).

## 1. Structure

```
Sale (document) ── number, status (posted | voided), branch, register, cashier session, user,
│                  customer?, tax_invoice, totals, cash_rounding, posted_at, voided_at?
├── SaleLine ── variant, unit, qty, unit_price, price_source, override?, line_discount,
│               cart_discount_share, tax_rate, tax, amount, scale_label?
├── SaleTender ── tender type, kind, amount, cash_received?, change_given?, change_saved?, reference?
└── (ledgers) stock movements (D9), customer balance entries (2.5 §11)

Return (document) ── number, status (posted | voided), original sale?, branch, register, session,
│                    user, customer?, reason, totals, exchange sale?
├── ReturnLine ── original line?, variant, unit, qty, refund amount, restock | write_off
└── ReturnTender ── tender type, kind, amount, reference?

HeldCart ── id, branch, register, user, customer?, lines (the cart as the register had it),
            held_at, note?, taken_by?
TenderType (HQ master data) ── name, kind, active, sort, opens drawer?, needs reference?
```

- A sale is created **posted**: there is no stored draft (D1). The cart in progress is not a
  document. A voided sale stays, with its reversal entries.
- Numbers use the branch-local date (2.1 §1):
  - Sale: `JKT01-R2-261116-0001` (per register, per local day, 4 digits or more).
  - Return: `JKT01-R2-RT-261116-0001`.
  - The numbers are allocated in the posting transaction, with the same retry rules as cashier
    sessions (2.1 §5).

## 2. Cart

- **Adding items:**
  - by barcode scan or typed code (C5 lookup)
  - by product search (F2)
  - by scale label (2.2 §6)
  - open-price items ask for the price (2.3 §5)
  - an archived product, a product blocked for the branch, or a `no_price` item without
    `price.override` → the matching error, and nothing is added
- **Qty:** typed before the scan (`3*` then scan) or changed on the line. Range > 0 to 99 999.999,
  with 3 decimals for weighed items and whole numbers otherwise (2.2 units). Max **300 lines** per cart.
- **Unit:** a barcode picks its unit; the line's unit can be changed (F-key on the line) to any
  unit of the product.
- **Pricing:** every change goes through the quote (2.3, `POST /pricing/quote`) for the whole cart,
  so quantity tiers that count the variant across lines stay right. `price.changed` re-prices an
  open cart, except overridden lines (2.3 §6).
- **Customer:** attaching or detaching a customer re-prices the cart (2.3 R5). Attaching is
  allowed until payment is confirmed.
- **Line view:** name + variant, qty + unit, unit price (struck through when an override or
  discount applies), discount, amount. The totals panel shows subtotal, discounts, tax (when
  tax-exclusive), total and item count.
- **Price check** (F7): look up an item's price without adding it to the cart.

## 3. Line actions

| Action | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| change qty / unit | `sale.create` | lowering qty is not a void |
| delete a line (void line) | `sale.void_line` (overridable) | reason required; audited; counted in the close report |
| manual price (override) | `price.override` MaxPct | 2.3 §6 |
| line discount (% or Rp) | `sale.discount` MaxPct | the % is checked against the line amount before discount |
| clear the whole cart (void cart) | `sale.void` (overridable) | reason required; audited with every line; counted in the close report |

- **Every removed line is a void**, even one scanned by mistake a second ago: it needs
  `sale.void_line`, a reason, and is counted in the close report. Lowering a qty is not a void.
- Void reasons are fixed in code for now: `customer_changed_mind`, `wrong_item`, `price_dispute`,
  `other` (with text).

## 4. Hold and resume

- **Hold** (F11 in §13): the cart, its customer and an optional note are stored on the branch
  server as a `HeldCart`. The register starts a new empty cart. Event `cart.held` (branch).
- **Resume list:** every held cart of the branch (any register, any cashier), newest first, showing
  register, cashier, time, customer, item count, total and note.
- **Resume** takes the cart: `POST /held-carts/{id}/take` → it is removed from the list. A second
  register taking it at the same time → `409 cart_taken`. The cart is re-quoted on resume (prices
  may have changed); overridden lines keep their price and the approval they had.
- Resuming needs an empty current cart (or hold the current one first).
- Held carts survive a restart. They are branch-local: not synced to HQ, and not documents.
- Held carts are **never dropped** automatically: they stay until resumed or deleted.
- Holding needs `sale.create`. Deleting a held cart without resuming it needs `sale.void`.

## 5. Payment

- **Pay** opens the payment panel. F4 starts with **cash**, F9 with a **non-cash** tender list.
  Split payment: add tenders until the rest is 0.
- **Tender rules:**
  - `cash`: the cashier types the cash received; change = received − rest. Only cash may exceed the
    rest. The drawer opens.
  - `card` / `qris` / `transfer` / `ewallet` / `other`: the amount defaults to the rest and cannot
    exceed it. A reference (approval code, last 4 digits) is required when the tender type says so.
  - `customer_balance`: needs an attached customer; amount ≤ balance (2.5 §11) → otherwise
    `422 insufficient_balance`.
- **Cash rounding** (2.3 §8): applies only when cash settles the rest. The rounded difference is
  stored on the sale as `cash_rounding` and shown on the receipt.
- **Saving change** (2.5 §11): after a cash tender with change and an attached customer, the
  cashier may save part or all of the change (F8). Limits and errors as in 2.5.
- **Zero total** (everything discounted, or only free items): posts with no tender.
- **Confirm** posts the sale (`PUT /sales/{id}`). The server repeats the quote and every permission
  check. If the totals differ (a price changed in between) → `409 cart_stale` with the fresh quote;
  the register shows the new total and asks again.
- After posting: the receipt (§9), the change due in large digits, then a new empty cart.

## 6. Totals, discounts, tax (in `core`, pure)

Order for each line:
1. `gross = unit_price × qty`, rounded half up to Rp 1 (2.3).
2. `line_discount` (manual, §3) → `after_line = gross − line_discount`.
3. **Cart discount** (% or Rp) is split over the lines in proportion to `after_line`. Remainders
   go by the largest remainder, ties to the earlier line, so the shares sum exactly.
4. `amount = after_line − cart_discount_share` (never below 0).
5. Tax per line on `amount` (2.3 §7), then summed.

- A price-embedded scale label (2.3 §5) is never discounted by a cart discount; it still takes a
  line discount.
- `sale.cart_discount` MaxPct is checked against the cart total before the cart discount.
- Total = Σ amount (+ Σ tax when tax-exclusive) + cash_rounding.

## 7. Voids after payment (post-void)

- `POST /sales/{id}/void {reason, refund}` with `sale.post_void` (overridable), at any time (D10).
  The voiding user needs an open cashier session on this register (`409 no_open_session`).
- **Money (D10, D11):** the part paid with the customer balance goes back to the balance
  (`payment_reversed`), and saved change is taken back (`change_saved_reversed`). Everything else
  is paid back in **cash** from the current drawer or to the **customer balance** (a customer must
  be attached; `max_balance` applies, and the rest is paid in cash).
- The cash paid out counts in the **current** session (`cash refunds`), never in the original one,
  whose closed report stays as it was.
- Stock movements are reversed.
- The sale keeps its number with status `voided`. The receipt reprints with **VOID**.
- A sale that has a return cannot be voided → `409 has_returns`.

## 8. Returns and exchanges

- **By receipt** (`refund.with_receipt`): the cashier scans the receipt's barcode/QR or types its
  number, or picks it from the customer's recent receipts (2.5 §9).
  - Only posted sales; sales from **another branch** only once they are on this branch (after
    sync, so in P1 multi-branch: the same branch only). A no-receipt return works anywhere.
  - Each line can be returned up to `sold − already returned`.
  - The refund per line is what the customer paid for it: `amount` (incl. cart discount share)
    × returned qty ÷ sold qty, rounded half up; the last return of a line takes what is left, so
    the line's refunds sum exactly to its amount.
  - Return window: company setting `return_days` (default **30**, `0` = no limit). Older → needs a
    manager override.
- **Without receipt** (`refund.no_receipt` MaxAmount): items are scanned; the refund is the current
  resolved price for the attached customer (or walk-in). Above the limit → manager override.
- **Refund tenders (D11):** cash or the customer balance only, whatever the sale was paid with.
  Cash comes out of the drawer (it lowers expected cash). A refund to the balance may exceed
  `max_saved_per_sale` but not `max_balance` (the rest is paid in cash). The part of the original
  sale paid with the balance goes back to the balance first.
- **Restock or write-off** per line (D9): restock posts a `return` stock movement; write-off posts
  `return` + `write_off` movements, with a reason.
- **Exchange (D12):** a return whose refund becomes an `exchange` tender on a new sale in the same
  flow. If the new sale costs more, the customer pays the difference; if less, the rest is refunded.
- Returns need an open cashier session (2.1) and are audited.

## 9. Receipt

The receipt text is built by `core` as fixed-width lines (32, 42 or 48 columns, a branch setting),
so the screen preview and the ESC/POS printer (2.11) print the same thing. Until 2.11, the register
shows it on screen and `GET /sales/{id}/receipt` returns it.

Contents, in order:
- branch receipt header (2.1), branch name, address, NPWP when tax-registered
- number, local date and time, register, cashier
- customer line (2.5 §10), or the full company block for a tax invoice sale (D13)
- lines: name, qty × unit price, discount, amount
- subtotal, discounts, tax (`PPN 11%` + base, or "incl. PPN Rp X" when tax-inclusive), cash rounding, total
- tenders with references masked to the last 4 characters, cash received, change, saved change and new balance (2.5)
- branch footer, plus a barcode/QR of the sale number (used by returns, §8)
- reprints say **COPY**, and voided sales say **VOID**

**Reprint:** the last sale of the current session needs no permission; any other sale needs the new
permission `sale.reprint`. Every reprint is audited.

## 10. Cashier session effects

`expected_cash` (2.1 §5) becomes:
`opening_cash + cash received − cash change given − cash refunds + cash_in − cash_out − payouts`.
Cash received minus change given is the cash that stays in the drawer, so it already holds the cash
rounding (e.g. 70 000 − 6 500 = 63 500 = 63 450 + 50) and the saved change (2.5 §11). Cash rounding
is shown on its own line in the report but not added again (§19 item 19). Cash refunds are paid
after their own cash rounding (§19 item 11).

The close report adds, from these documents:
- per tender type: count + amount (sales and refunds separately)
- count + amount of: line voids, cart voids, post-voids, returns, discounts (line and cart), price overrides
- saved change, balance paid, and cash rounding

## 11. Permissions, sync, events

- Existing: `sale.create`, `sale.discount`, `sale.cart_discount`, `sale.void_line`, `sale.void`,
  `sale.post_void`, `price.override`, `refund.with_receipt`, `refund.no_receipt`, `drawer.open`.
- New: `sale.reprint` (seed: Owner, Manager), `tender.manage` (HQ: tender types; seed: Owner).
- Sales, returns, their ledgers and audit entries are **branch transactional data**: written with
  their outbox event in one transaction, pushed to HQ (plan §1). Tender types are HQ master data.
- Sales use the branch node's data only, so selling never waits for HQ.
- Events: `cart.held` / `cart.taken` (branch audience, refreshes the resume list), `sale.posted`
  (branch, for the dashboard later).

## 19. Clarifications (from S1, 2026-11-16)

From S1 (`core::sale`):

1. **Quantities:** ≤ 0, above 99 999.999, or decimals on an integer unit → `422 invalid_qty` (the
   catalog's code). A price-embedded label without a store price has qty 0 ("left empty", 2.3 §5);
   it is accepted and moves no stock.
2. **Rescan (D8):** the scan is added to the newest line with the same variant + unit that may
   merge. A line with a discount still merges (the discount stays). A merge above 99 999.999 is
   refused (`invalid_qty`), not split. A full cart (300 lines) still takes a merging rescan.
3. **Line edits:** a price label's qty and unit cannot change, and a scale label's unit cannot
   either (`invalid_field`, `line`). Changing the unit of an overridden line drops the override:
   the line is listed again and re-quoted. Open-price and scale-label lines cannot be overridden.
4. **Discount amounts:** a % is at most 100%. An Rp line discount above the line's gross (e.g.
   after lowering the qty) counts as the gross. An Rp cart discount above the lines that take it
   counts as their sum; a % cart discount is a % of those lines (price labels excluded).
5. **Permission values:** discount and override percentages are rounded **up** to a basis point
   (10.005% is checked as 10.01%), like 2.3 §6. `sale.cart_discount` is checked against Σ
   `after_line` of **all** lines (labels included). A discount that comes to Rp 0 needs no check.
   Raising a price is `price.override` with 0%; without a resolved price no limit applies.
6. **Cash:** cash that covers the rounded rest settles the sale; the rounding difference becomes
   `cash_rounding` and the excess is change. Cash below the exact rest is a partial payment
   without rounding. Cash between the exact and the rounded-up rest is refused (`invalid_field`,
   `amount`). If a "down" rounding takes the rest to 0, cash 0 settles it. The settling cash
   tender stores the rounded rest as its `amount`, so Σ tender amounts = total + `cash_rounding`
   and `change_given + change_saved = cash_received − amount`.
7. **Other tenders:** amount ≤ 0 or an inactive tender type → `invalid_field`. A balance tender
   without an attached customer → `invalid_field` (`customer`). References are trimmed, at most 64
   characters. Several balance tenders on one sale share the balance.
8. **Saved change:** saved from the change of the last cash tender; saving 0 undoes it. The room
   under `max_balance` counts the balance this sale already pays with.
9. **Exchange tender:** kind `exchange`, without a tender type. It is not a tender type kind (D4),
   so HQ cannot create one.
10. **Refund to the balance:** a new balance entry kind `refund` (+) for the part of a return or
    post-void paid to the balance (extends 2.5 §11's table; the S2 migration adds it to the
    `kind` check). The part of the sale paid with the balance goes back first as
    `payment_reversed`; the `max_balance` cap is applied after that.
11. **Cash rounding on refunds:** the cash part of a return or post-void is rounded with the
    company setting (2.3 §8: "down" rounds toward zero, in the shop's favour), and the difference
    is stored as the document's `cash_rounding`.
12. **Returns:** a partial return never refunds more than what is left of the line (many small
    returns could otherwise overshoot by rounding); the last return takes exactly what is left.
    A return does not reverse saved change; only a post-void does. Returning a voided sale →
    `409 conflict`; an imported old-Sultan sale → `invalid_field` (`sale`).
13. **Return window:** counted in branch-local calendar days: a sale dated at most `return_days`
    days before today is inside. Outside → `return_window`, which the server turns into a manager
    override request.
14. **Post-void:** the customer gets back what they handed over: balance tenders go back to the
    balance, saved change is reversed (`change_saved_reversed`) and paid out with the rest (cash
    or balance). A second void → `409 conflict`.
15. **Stock movement kinds:** `sale` (−), `sale_void` (+), `return` (+), `write_off` (−), one per
    line and variant; a bundle's components are summed per variant within the line.
16. **Tax invoice switch:** needs an attached customer with a tax id (`invalid_field`,
    `tax_invoice`); detaching the customer, or switching to one without a tax id, turns it off.
17. **Reasons:** the `other` void reason and a write-off reason are 1–200 characters (trimmed).
18. **Receipt layout:** labels are keys translated by the caller; amounts in columns have no
    currency and use `.` grouping (`1.250.000`), quantities a decimal comma without trailing zeros
    (`1,255`, `2`). Each line prints its gross and its line discount; the totals' discount is line +
    cart. Tax-inclusive: `incl. PPN 11%` + tax per rate; tax-exclusive: `DPP` (base) and
    `PPN 11%` per rate. `Change` is the change handed back; saved change and the new balance follow
    on one line when it fits, else two. The local date and time print as `16/11/2026 14:05`.
    COPY is printed at the top, VOID at the top and the bottom.
19. **Expected cash (§10):** "cash tenders − cash change given" is the cash that stays in the
    drawer, `cash_received − change_given` per cash tender. That already holds the cash rounding
    and the saved change, so `cash_rounding` is reported but not added a second time.

    renames, deactivates or archives them. The balance needs a tender type of its own because only
    the exchange tender has none (§19 item 9).
21. **Tender type edits:** the kind never changes once created (`invalid_field`, `kind`); an
    archived tender type cannot be edited (`409 archived`); archiving twice changes nothing.
22. **Stock location (D9):** the register's default location, else the branch's receiving default,
    else any location of the branch, else none (the movement is stored without a location). A
    post-void puts the stock back where the sale took it. There is no cached balance yet: on hand is
    the sum of the ledger per branch and variant (2.8 adds caching and costing).
23. **Idempotent posting (§12):** the server hashes the posted body; the same id with the same hash
    returns the stored sale or return, another hash is `409 conflict`. An imported sale has no hash,
    so any `PUT` to its id is a conflict.
24. **Numbers:** sales and returns each have their own sequence per register and branch-local day.
    An imported sale keeps its old number as `number` and `legacy_number` and has no sequence;
    `GET /sales/by-number/{n}` finds it by either.
25. **Exchange:** the new sale is posted first, in the return's transaction, with its own sale
    number; the return then points at it. An `exchange` tender is only accepted on that sale.
26. **Return reason:** the return's own `reason` (§1) is an optional note of at most 200
    characters; the write-off reason per line stays required (§19 item 17).
27. **Refund by receipt:** the database recomputes each line's refund with `line_refund` from the
    stored line (its `total`, so with tax when tax-exclusive, §19 item 12) and what earlier returns
    took, and refuses another amount (`invalid_field`, `refund`). Balance entries need a customer on
    the document (`invalid_field`, `customer`).
28. **Post-void tenders:** one cash tender for the cash paid out and one balance tender for
    `payment_reversed + refund`; the stored refund is what the customer handed over (tenders +
    saved change). The void records the paying session (D10).
29. **Session figures:** a sale's tenders count in the session it was posted in, even when it is
    voided later; the void adds `PostVoid` (the sale's total) and its refunds to the voiding
    session. A return adds its refund and one refund per tender, the exchange tender included (it
    nets against the exchange sale's tender). A price override counts `resolved × qty − gross`
    (at least 0; 0 without a resolved price). Line and cart voids come from their audit entries
    (S3), cash in/out and payouts from their own records.
30. **Purchase history (2.5 §9):** sales, **voided ones included and marked VOID** (decided
    2026-11-17), and posted returns, including customers merged into this one; a sale shows what was paid (`total + cash_rounding`),
    a return its refund; newest first by posting time. The till's recent receipts (used to start a
    return) are **posted** sales only, since the caller's cut-off (90 days), 5 by default.
31. **Held carts:** holding again overwrites a cart that still waits; a taken cart stays stored
    with who took it, so a late taker gets `cart_taken` (not `not_found`), and it can no longer be
    held again or deleted. Deleting is a real delete with an audit entry (`held_cart.delete`) that
    keeps the cart; holding and taking are not audited (events only, §11).
32. **Audit and outbox:** `sale.post`, `sale.void`, `return.post` (audit) with `sale.posted`,
    `sale.voided`, `return.posted` (outbox, with lines, tenders and stock movements);
    `tender_type.put` / `tender_type.archive` for both. Balance entries keep their own
    `customer.balance_entry` events. Posting marks the sold variants (and bundle components) used.
33. **`sale.reprint` and `tender.manage`** have no limit. `sale.reprint` **takes a manager
    override** at the till like the voids (decided 2026-11-17); `tender.manage` is HQ back office
    only and not overridable.
34. **Balance payment on a return (§8):** the part of the sale paid with the balance
    (`payment_reversed`) goes back to the sale's own customer, so a return by receipt that
    reverses a balance payment must carry the sale's customer (`invalid_field`, `customer`); a
    return without a receipt has no balance payment to reverse (`invalid_field`,
    `balance_entries`). A `refund` to the balance of a sale **with a customer** goes only to that
    customer (decided 2026-11-17), so money is never moved to another person's balance. A sale
    without a customer, or a return without a receipt, may refund to the customer attached to the
    return.
35. **Voiding an exchange sale (§7, D11):** the part paid with the exchange tender is what the
    customer handed over (the returned goods), so a post-void pays it back in cash or to the
    balance like any other tender (decided 2026-11-17). The return that made the exchange stays
    posted.

### From S3 (`server`, 2026-11-17)

36. **Overrides at posting:** one request may need several approvals (a line discount, a cart
    discount, a price override): `X-Override` carries one token per approval, comma separated.
    Each check takes a token approved for exactly its permission, value and `context_ref`. The
    first check without one answers `403 needs_override` for it; tokens that match nothing answer
    `403 invalid_override`. `context_ref` is the sale ID (`PUT /sales/{id}`, post-void, reprint),
    the return ID (`PUT /returns/{id}`; the exchange sale's own checks use the exchange sale ID),
    the cart ID (`POST /cart-voids`) and the held cart ID (`DELETE /held-carts/{id}`). The approver
    of a price override is stored on the line (`override_by`).
37. **Posted body (§5):** lines name variant, unit and quantity with an optional `typed_price`
    (the open price, or an override), `discount` (`pct_bp` or `amount`) and `scale_label`
    (`weight`, or `price` with `label_amount`); `cart_discount`; tenders `{tender_type_id, amount,
    reference}` (cash: the cash received); `save_change`; and the `totals` the register showed
    (`subtotal`, `line_discount`, `cart_discount`, `tax`, `total`). Any difference from the server's
    totals is `409 cart_stale` with `quote: {totals, lines}`. Lines are kept as posted (no merging
    at the server). A replay is recognised before anything else (session, quote), so the same
    body always answers the stored document; the hash is SHA-256 of the body's JSON.
38. **Line and cart voids reach the server** through `POST /cart-voids {kind: line|cart,
    cart_id, reason, reason_text?, lines}` (`Idempotency-Key`; `sale.void_line` / `sale.void`,
    overridable; an open session of the caller on this register). The server writes the audit
    entry `sale.void_line` / `sale.void_cart` on the open cashier session with the amount, and
    the close report counts them from there. Dropping a held cart (`DELETE /held-carts/{id}`) is
    audited as `held_cart.delete` and is not counted as a cart void (the cart may belong to
    another session).
39. **Return window (§8):** `return_days` has no stored setting yet: every node uses 30. Outside
    the window → `403 return_window` (`days`, `permission: refund.with_receipt`); a manager's
    override of `refund.with_receipt` (no value, `context_ref` = the return ID) lets the return
    through, even for a cashier who holds the permission.
40. **This branch only:** a return by receipt and a post-void take only sales of the register's
    branch (`422 invalid_field`, `sale`); held carts of another branch answer `404`.
41. **Balance part of a return by receipt:** what goes back to the balance first is the sale's
    balance tenders less what earlier returns gave back, i.e. less `min(balance paid, Σ earlier
    refunds of the sale's lines)`, since every return reverses the balance part first.
42. **No-receipt refund:** today's resolved price of the item for the attached customer (or
    walk-in) × qty, with tax when tax-exclusive; `refund.no_receipt` is checked with the total
    refund. Archived products may still be returned.
43. **Exchange (D12):** the exchange tender is `min(refund, new sale total)`; what is left of the
    refund is planned with `plan_refund` (balance part first, then cash or balance).
44. **Refund tender types:** post-voids and returns pay with the first active `cash` and
    `customer_balance` tender types, else the seeded Tunai and Saldo pelanggan.
45. **Who reads sales and returns:** the document's cashier, or anyone holding `sale.create` or
    `report.sales` at its branch (till staff look receipts up). `GET /sales` and `GET /returns`
    need `report.sales` at the `branch_id` filter (everywhere without one), except a list of the
    caller's own cashier session (`cashier_session_id`, the till's last receipts).
46. **Receipt (§9):** English labels for now (the client translates later); the width is
    `?width=32|42|48` (no branch setting yet, default 32). The saved-change line prints the
    customer's current balance (a reprint does not know the balance at the time of the sale).
    `copy=true` is audited as `sale.reprint` (with the approver); it is free for the last sale of
    the caller's open session on this register; from the back office it needs `sale.reprint`
    without an override.
47. **Paths:** the till's recent receipts are `GET /customers/{id}/recent-receipts?days=90&limit=5`
    (posted sales only); purchase history pages with `before=<posted_at ms>_<id>` (the
    `next_before_id` of the previous page).
48. **Close report (§10):** the report gains a `sales` block (`core::sale::session_report`: cash
    received, change, refunds, rounding, per tender type, line and cart voids, post-voids,
    returns, discounts, overrides, saved change, balance paid). It is computed on read from the
    session's documents, which never change after the close (a later post-void counts in the
    voiding session), so the stored report needs no new columns and old reports read as before.
    Expected cash follows §10 (`movements::SalesMovements`): cash tenders count `cash_received −
    change_given`, cash refunds lower it, payouts are cash out. The 2.1 tender table keeps cash,
    card, QRIS and transfer; e-wallet, other, balance and exchange tenders appear in the `sales`
    block only.
49. **Events:** `sale.posted` (also for an exchange sale) and `sale.voided`, `cart.held`,
    `cart.taken` (also after a held cart is dropped, so resume lists refresh), all branch
    audience.
50. **Posting checks the catalog** for archived products and variants (`422 archived`); branch
    blocks are checked by the lookup when the item is added, not again at posting. A price label
    without a store price posts with unit price 0 and qty 0.
