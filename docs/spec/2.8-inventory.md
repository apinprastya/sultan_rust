# 2.8 Inventory — spec

Status: **agreed** (2026-11-26). §0 lists every decision as agreed with the user: I1 changed to
**everything in plan §2.8** (so expiry lots and suggested purchase orders from sales velocity are
in, I13 and I21–I22), all others as proposed.
Parent: `docs/plan.md` §2.8. Depends on 2.1 (branches, stock locations, permissions, overrides,
audit, outbox), 2.2 (product types, units, bundles), 2.3 (tax classes), 2.6 (the sales stock
ledger, D9) and 2.7 (the pattern for HQ-owned reasons). 2.9 (money: paying suppliers, payables
aging, supplier credits), 2.10 (stock and profit reports) and the old-Sultan importer build on it.

**What exists today** (so this spec only adds):
- `stock_location` per branch (one is the receiving default) and a register's default location
  (2.1 §1). Moving stock between locations of one branch is an internal transfer (2.1 §1).
- An append-only `stock_movement` ledger written by sales and returns (2.6 D9, §19 items 15 and
  22): kinds `sale` (−), `sale_void` (+), `return` (+), `write_off` (−), in base units, at the
  register's default location. Negative stock is allowed. There is no cached balance and no cost.
- Product types `stock`, `weighed`, `service`, `bundle` (2.2 §5): a bundle moves its components'
  stock, a service has no stock. Units with factors convert to the base (stock) unit (2.2 §4).
- Permission `stock.adjust` (Owner only); `report.profit` exists for cost and margin.
- No sync worker yet: separate branch nodes do not exchange data with HQ (plan §1, later in P1).

## 1. Structure

```
Supplier (HQ) ── SupplierItem (supplier, variant, supplier code, last price, preferred)
StockReason (HQ) ── name, direction (in | out | both), active, sort
Branch
├── StockBalance   (location, variant) → on hand                       cached, §4
├── StockCost      (variant) → qty, value, reference cost              cached, §5
├── StockLot       (location, variant, batch, expiry) → qty            cached, §9
├── ReorderRule    (variant) → min, max                                §10
└── documents      draft → posted (→ closed | voided where stated)
    ├── PurchaseOrder ── lines (variant, unit, qty, price)            -PO-
    ├── GoodsReceipt  ── lines (variant, unit, qty, price, discount, batch, expiry, PO line?)  -GR-
    │   └── Payable (amount, due date, paid?)                          → paid in 2.9
    ├── SupplierReturn ── lines (variant, unit, qty, price, receipt line?)        -RS-
    ├── Transfer (to branch) ── lines (variant, unit, qty shipped, qty received)  -TR-
    ├── InternalTransfer (from location → to location) ── lines                   -IT-
    ├── Adjustment ── lines (variant, unit, qty ±, reason, unit cost?)            -ADJ-
    ├── Opname (location, full | cycle) ── lines (variant, snapshot, counted)     -OP-
    └── OpeningStock (location) ── lines (variant, unit, qty, unit cost, batch, expiry)  -OS-
StockMovement (append-only ledger; §2)
```

- Suppliers, supplier items and stock reasons are **HQ-owned** master data (branch nodes answer
  writes with `403 read_only_replica`). Documents, balances, costs, lots and reorder rules belong to
  the **branch** (an HQ user may write them for any branch when both are in one database).
- Every document stores `branch_id`, `user_id`, `created_at`, `posted_at`, `posted_by`, a note
  (0–500 characters) and, when an override was used, `approved_by`. Posted documents are never
  edited; the ones that can be undone say so.
- Quantities on lines are in the line's **unit** (any unit of the product, 2.2 §4) and converted to
  base units (3 dp) when posted. Services and bundles cannot be on an inventory line
  (`invalid_field`, `variant`); a bundle is bought and counted as its components.

## 2. Stock movements

Every stock change is one row in `stock_movement` (append-only, base units, signed). 2.8 adds
`value` (signed Rupiah, null for rows written before 2.8), `lot_id` (null when untracked) and the
document references. Kinds:

| Kind | Sign | Written by | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sale` / `sale_void` | − / + | sale, post-void (2.6) | at the average cost (§5); a void puts back exactly what the sale took |
| `return` / `write_off` | + / − | return (2.6) | a restock comes back at the value the sale line took (else the average); a write-off is valued at the average |
| `receipt` | + | goods receipt | the line's landed cost (§6) |
| `supplier_return` | − | supplier return | the average (§7) |
| `transfer_out` / `transfer_in` | − / + | transfer ship / receive | the sender's average; the receiver gets the shipped value |
| `transit_loss` | − | transfer receive (shortage) | posted at the **sender**, the shipped value of the missing quantity |
| `internal_out` / `internal_in` | − / + | internal transfer | the average (no change in cost) |
| `adjust` | ± | adjustment | out: the average; in: the average, or the typed unit cost when there is none |
| `count` | ± | opname | as `adjust` |
| `opening` | + | opening stock | the typed unit cost |

- A movement's location is the document's location; sales keep 2.6's rule.
- Bundles: each component moves with its own cost; the sale line's cost is their sum.
- Writes happen in the document's posting transaction, with the balance, cost and lot updates, the
  audit entry and the outbox event (one transaction per write).

## 3. Suppliers

- Fields: `code` (1–20, upper case, unique), `name` (1–120), address, phone, email, `tax_id`
  (NPWP, optional), `pkp` (bool, informational), `terms_days` (0–365, default 0 = cash),
  `lead_days` (0–365, default 7, used by §10), notes, `active`; archived like other master data.
- **Supplier items** (supplier, variant): `supplier_code` (optional, unique per supplier),
  `last_price` (per base unit, set by every posted receipt), `preferred` (at most one supplier per
  variant). A receipt line for a variant that has no supplier item creates one.

## 4. On hand

- **On hand** of (branch, location, variant) is the sum of the ledger; it is cached in
  `stock_balance` and updated in the same transaction. Branch on hand = sum over its locations.
- A migration builds the cache from the existing ledger (2.6 rows without a location go to the
  branch's receiving default, else to a location created for them).
- **On order** = ordered − received over the branch's open POs. **In transit** = shipped − (received
  + lost) over the branch's open outgoing transfers (shown at both ends).
- Negative on hand is allowed (I7). A back-office document whose posting would leave any line
  below zero shows a warning before posting, never a refusal.

## 5. Cost (moving weighted average, per branch and variant)

State per (branch, variant): `qty` (base, 3 dp, all locations), `value` (Rupiah) and the
**reference cost** `ref = (ref_value, ref_qty)`, the last known average as a fraction.

- **Incoming at a cost** (`receipt`, `opening`, `transfer_in`, an `adjust` / `count` increase with
  a typed cost) with quantity q and value v:
  - if `qty > 0`: `value += v`, `qty += q`;
  - if `qty ≤ 0` (none or negative): the negative part is first settled at the new unit cost
    (`value = (qty + q) × v / q`, rounded half up, or 0 while `qty + q ≤ 0`), `qty += q`. So stock
    that was sold short takes the new price, and the old sales keep their recorded value.
  - Then `ref = (value, qty)` when `qty > 0`, else `ref = (v, q)`.
- **Outgoing** (and incoming at the average) with quantity q: the value is
  `round_half_up(q × ref_value / ref_qty)`; `qty` and `value` change by it. When `qty` reaches 0,
  `value` is set to 0 and the rounding difference is kept on that movement. `ref` stays.
- **No cost yet** (`ref` unset: a product never received, or stock only from before 2.8): the
  movement's value is **null** ("no cost"), and reports show it as unknown, never as 0. An `adjust`
  or `count` increase then needs a typed unit cost (`invalid_field`, `unit_cost`), else it posts
  without cost.
- The unit cost shown anywhere is `ref_value / ref_qty` per base unit, rounded to Rp 1 for display
  (per unit of the line when shown on a line).
- Cost is per branch: a transfer carries the sender's value to the receiver (§8).

## 6. Purchasing

### Purchase order (optional)
- `draft → ordered → closed`, or `draft → cancelled`. Supplier, order date, expected date, location
  to receive into (default: the receiving default), lines (variant, unit, qty > 0, price per unit
  from the supplier's last price, editable).
- **Ordered** (`purchase.order`): numbered, printable later (2.11); the text shows supplier codes.
- A receipt may fill any part of its lines; a line can be over-received (a warning). The PO
  **closes** by itself when every line is fully received, or by hand ("close the rest"). An
  ordered PO with nothing received can be **cancelled**; a closed one cannot reopen.

### Goods receipt
- `draft → posted → voided`. Supplier, location, the supplier's **invoice number** (required,
  unique per supplier among posted receipts: `409 conflict`) and **invoice date**, **payment**:
  `credit` (due date = invoice date + the supplier's `terms_days`, editable) or `cash` (paid now),
  **tax mode**: `included` · `added` · `none`, tax rate (default the company's standard PPN rate),
  **header discount** (Rp), **other costs** (Rp), lines.
- Lines: variant, unit, qty > 0, price per unit (before discount, in the receipt's tax mode), line
  discount (Rp, ≤ the line amount), PO line (optional), and for a tracked product (§9) a batch
  number (0–40 characters) and an **expiry date** (required).
- Lines can be added by scanning; the same variant and unit adds up unless it has a different
  batch or expiry.
- **Totals:** line net = qty × price − line discount; the header discount is spread over the lines
  by line net; tax: `included` → net already holds PPN, base = net × 100 / (100 + rate); `added` →
  PPN = base × rate / 100 on top; `none` → no tax. Invoice total = Σ net − header discount (+ PPN when
  added). Spreads use the largest-remainder method, so the parts add up to the whole exactly.
- **Landed cost** of a line = its net after the header discount, **without PPN when the company is
  PKP** (I5), **with PPN otherwise**, plus its share of the other costs (spread by the same line
  value). That is the `receipt` movement's value; the unit cost on screen is value / base qty.
- **Posting** (`purchase.receive`): stock in, cost (§5), lots (§9), supplier items' `last_price`,
  the PO lines' received quantities, and a **payable** {supplier, receipt, amount = invoice total,
  due date, paid = (payment = cash)}. The screen shows, per line, the new unit cost against the
  variant's Default price and the margin, with a jump to F7 Pricing (I14).
- **Void** (`purchase.receive` + manager override): only while no later movement of any of its
  variants has used its cost (no sale, transfer, return or adjustment since, at that branch), and
  its payable is unpaid; otherwise `409 receipt_in_use` and the user posts a supplier return. A
  void writes the reversing movements and voids the payable.

### Supplier return
- `draft → posted`. Supplier, location, lines (variant, unit, qty > 0, price per unit; when taken
  from a receipt, its line and price, and at most what that line received minus earlier returns:
  `invalid_field`, `qty`).
- Posting (`purchase.receive`): stock out valued at the **average** (the average itself does not
  change); the supplier credit (Σ qty × price, with the receipt's tax mode) is stored on the return
  for 2.9 to set against the payable. A tracked product leaves from the receipt line's lot when
  given, else FEFO.

## 7. Adjustments and opening stock

- **Stock reasons** (HQ data, written with `stock.adjust` at HQ): name (1–60), direction
  `in` · `out` · `both`, active, sort. Seeded: Rusak / damaged (out), Kedaluwarsa / expired (out),
  Hilang / lost (out), Sampel (out), Pemakaian sendiri / own use (out), Koreksi / correction (both).
  A reason cannot change direction once used; inactive reasons cannot be picked.
- **Adjustment** (`stock.adjust`): location, lines (variant, unit, signed qty ≠ 0, reason whose
  direction allows the sign, note when the reason is Koreksi). Posted at once (no draft needed, but a
  draft can be kept). Increases are valued at the average, or at a typed unit cost when there is no
  cost yet (§5). Not voidable: a mistake is corrected by another adjustment.
- **Opening stock** (`stock.adjust`): location, lines (variant, unit, qty > 0, unit cost ≥ 0,
  batch / expiry for tracked products). Posts `opening` movements at the typed cost. Allowed at any
  time (a new branch, a forgotten product, the importer).

## 8. Transfers

### Between branches (I8)
- `requested → shipped → received`, or `requested → cancelled`; a transfer can also start at
  `shipped` (no request). From branch + location → to branch + location (default the receiving
  default).
- **Request** (`stock.transfer` at the receiving branch): lines (variant, unit, qty).
- **Ship** (`stock.transfer` at the sending branch): the shipped quantity per line (0 allowed when
  out of stock); posts `transfer_out` at the sender's average; tracked products leave FEFO and the
  lots travel with the line (batch, expiry, qty). Now **in transit**.
- **Receive** (`stock.transfer` at the receiving branch): the received quantity per line ≤ shipped
  (more → `invalid_field`, `qty`; an extra is an adjustment). Posts `transfer_in` at the receiver
  with the shipped value for the received part (the lots too), and a **`transit_loss`** at the
  **sender** for any shortage, valued from the shipment. Received in one step; the transfer is then
  closed.
- A shipped transfer cannot be cancelled; a wrong one is sent back with a new transfer.
- Until sync exists, both branches must be in the same database: a branch node shows "the other
  branch is on another server" (`409 other_node`) when asked to ship to a branch it does not hold.

### Inside a branch
- **Internal transfer** (`stock.transfer`): from location → to location, lines; posted at once as
  `internal_out` / `internal_in` pairs. Cost does not change; lots move FEFO.

## 9. Expiry lots (I21–I22)

- Product field **`track_expiry`** (catalog, HQ; can be switched on any time; switching it off keeps
  the lots for history but stops using them) and **`expiry_warn_days`** (optional; else the company
  setting, default 30).
- A **lot** is (branch, location, variant, batch, expiry). Receipts, opening stock and transfer
  receipts put quantity into lots; every other outgoing movement takes it **first-expiry-first-out**
  (earliest expiry, then oldest lot), recorded on the movement (`lot_id`, one movement per lot
  taken). When the lots are used up, the rest moves without a lot ("no lot" quantity can go
  negative).
- Incoming movements other than receipts (a sale void, a restocked return) go back to the lot they
  came from when known, else to "no lot".
- An **opname** of a tracked product counts totals: a shortage is taken FEFO from the lots, an
  excess is put into "no lot" (or a lot the counter types).
- **Expiring list** (F10): lots with qty > 0 whose expiry is within the warn days, and expired
  ones, per branch and location, with the value at the average; Enter opens an adjustment with the
  "expired" reason prefilled. Nothing blocks a sale (I22).

## 10. Reorder

- **Reorder rule** per (branch, variant): `min` and `max` (base units, `0 ≤ min ≤ max`); writes need
  `purchase.order`.
- **Low stock** list: branch on hand + on order ≤ min, and negative on hand.
- **Suggested PO** for a supplier (its preferred variants, or all it has supplied):
  - velocity = (sold − returned) base qty over the last **N** days (company setting, default 30) / N,
    from the ledger (`sale` + `sale_void` + `return`);
  - with a rule: when on hand + on order ≤ min, suggest max − (on hand + on order);
  - without a rule: suggest velocity × (supplier `lead_days` + **cover days**, company setting,
    default 14) − (on hand + on order), when that is > 0;
  - rounded **up** to whole units of the line's purchase unit (the supplier item's last unit, else the
    base unit). The user edits the lines, then saves a draft PO.

## 11. Opname (I9–I10)

- `draft (counting) → posted`, or `draft → cancelled`. A location and a scope: **full** (every stock
  product of the branch) or **cycle** (chosen products and / or categories).
- **Start** (`stock.count`): the snapshot of on hand per variant in scope is taken at that moment.
- **Counting**: scan or type; each scan adds to the line (a typed quantity replaces it), several
  users may count into one opname at once (each count is an entry; the line is their sum), counts
  in any unit of the product.
- **Variance** per counted line = counted − (snapshot + net movements of that variant at that
  location since the snapshot, other than this opname). It is shown live.
- **Posting** (`stock.count` **and** `stock.adjust`): `count` movements for every non-zero variance,
  valued as `adjust` (§5). In a full count, the uncounted variants with on hand ≠ 0 are listed and
  posting asks: **set them to 0** or **leave them** (I10). A cycle count never touches what was not
  counted.
- Only one open opname per location and variant at a time (`409 opname_open` naming it).

## 17. Test cases

`core` (table-driven):
1. Average: 10 @ 1 000 then 10 @ 1 300 → 20 @ 1 150; sell 5 → value 17 250, ref unchanged.
2. Rounding: 3 @ total 1 000, sell 1 → 333; sell 1 → 333; sell 1 → 334 and value 0 (no drift).
3. Negative: on hand −2 (sold with ref 1 000), receive 10 @ 1 200 → qty 8, value 9 600, ref 1 200.
4. No cost yet: sale value null; first receipt sets the ref; an adjust increase without cost and
   without a typed cost → `invalid_field`.
5. Receipt totals: included / added / none × header discount × other costs, the spreads add up to
   the whole (largest remainder); landed cost PKP vs non-PKP.
6. Units: 2 box(24) @ 48 000 → 48 base @ 2 000.

## 20. Readings (from N1/N2)

Readings made while building N1 (`core::stock`) and N2 (`crates/db/src/stock/`, migration
`20261126000001_inventory`), where the spec left a choice. Agreed rules are unchanged.

1. **Receipt void kind.** The reversing movements of a voided receipt are a new kind
   `receipt_void` (−), like `sale_void` for a sale: each line goes back out at its landed cost and
   from its lot (`CostState::reverse`), so the average returns to that of the remaining stock.
2. **Incoming at a recorded value** (a sale void, a restocked return, `transfer_in`) follows §5's
   "incoming at a cost" rule (the reference becomes the new average); without a recorded value
   (a row from before 2.8) it is incoming at the average.
3. **Stock without a cost.** Stock with a quantity but no cost yet (from before 2.8) is treated
   like the "none or negative" case when the first cost arrives: the stock after the receipt takes
   the new unit cost (`value = (qty + q) × v / q`).
4. **Increase without a cost.** An `adjust` increase while the variant has no cost needs a typed
   unit cost (`invalid_field`, `unit_cost`); an opname (`count`) increase posts without a cost
   (value null), which is the "else it posts without cost" of §5. With a cost, a typed unit cost is
   ignored (the average wins).
5. **Reaching 0.** Whenever a movement at the average brings the quantity to exactly 0 (out or in),
   the value is set to 0 and the remainder is that movement's value (no drift).
6. **Transit loss.** The shipped quantity already left the sender with `transfer_out`. So that the
   sender's on hand stays the ledger's sum, the shortage is posted at the sender as a pair at the
   shipped value of the missing part: `transfer_in` (+) then `transit_loss` (−), both at the
   sender's location and without a lot. The pair does not change the sender's average; the
   `transit_loss` row carries the loss for 2.10.
7. **Lots on a transfer.** The received part takes the travelling lots first-expiry-first-out and
   goes into the same batch and expiry at the receiver; the lost part's lots are not recreated.
   Quantities are given in the line's unit and stored in base units; a line left out ships (or
   receives) in full.
8. **Transfer numbers.** A transfer is numbered by the branch that creates it: the receiver for a
   request, the sender for one shipped at once (`-TR-` of that branch).
9. **Receipt in use.** "No later movement used its cost" is read strictly: any later movement of
   any of the receipt's variants at its branch (any kind, another receipt too) makes the receipt
   `receipt_in_use`. A cash receipt's payable is paid when posted, so a cash receipt cannot be
   voided (a supplier return instead).
10. **Last price.** A supplier item's `last_price` is the receipt line's price per base unit
    (price / unit factor, before discounts, in the receipt's tax mode, rounded to Rp 1); its
    `last_unit` is the line's unit, the purchase unit of suggestions.
11. **Supplier return tax mode and lots.** When lines come from a receipt, the credit uses the tax
    mode and rate of the first receipt named; otherwise the return's own. A line from a receipt
    leaves from that receipt line's lot (which may go below 0), otherwise FEFO. Several lines on
    the same receipt line add up against what it received minus earlier posted returns.
12. **Restocked returns.** A restock goes back into the lots the sale line took from, in the order
    taken (each up to what it gave), the rest without a lot; its value is the sale line's value ×
    returned / sold, spread over the parts by quantity. A written-off return goes in and out
    without a lot (the write-off at the average). A sale's movements record the sale line number
    when the per-line movements add up to the posted ones.
13. **Lots of adjustments and counts.** An adjustment decrease and an opname shortage take lots
    FEFO; increases go to "no lot" (typing a lot for an opname excess is left to N6).
14. **Drafts.** Purchase orders, goods receipts and supplier returns are stored as drafts, written
    whole by their `PUT`. Adjustments, opening stock and internal transfers post at once; their
    drafts stay on the client for now (the tables allow `draft` for later).
15. **Opname snapshot.** The snapshot is on hand at the start plus a ledger mark (a `UUIDv7` taken
    then); "movements since" are the rows after the mark at that location, other than the opname's
    own. A full count covers every non-archived stock or weighed variant plus anything with stock at
    the location; a variant first counted later in a full count gets a line whose snapshot is its on
    hand at the mark. A cycle count by category takes the category's own products. One open opname
    per location and variant is a lock row (primary key) freed when posted or cancelled.
16. **Count entries.** A scan adds (it may be negative to take a mistaken scan back) and a typed
    quantity replaces; a line's count never goes below 0 (`invalid_field`, `qty`). An entry is
    stored once by its client ID.
17. **Locations.** A document without a location uses the branch's receiving default. The
    migration moves 2.6 rows without a location to the receiving default, else any live location,
    else a location "Stok lama" it creates. A sale at a branch without any location is balanced
    under the all-zero location ID.
18. **Product expiry fields.** `track_expiry` and `expiry_warn_days` are new product columns, set
    with `stock::set_product_expiry` until N3 adds them to the product `PUT`; the 2.2 `tracking`
    column is left as it is.
19. **Settings.** `pkp` is the inventory setting of §13 (default false), separate from the 2.3
    pricing setting `tax_registered`. `standard_ppn_rate` is stored (default 11%) rather than read
    from the Standard tax class; a receipt or supplier return without a rate takes it.
20. **Stock clerk.** §12 seeds `stock.adjust` for Owner and Manager only, so the migration takes
    it from the seeded Stock clerk of existing installs (it guarded nothing before 2.8).
21. **Audit and outbox.** The N2 repositories take the caller's transaction and do not append
    audit or outbox rows; the N3 routes add them (§15) in the same transaction.
22. **Reorder.** Velocity counts the ledger rows of kinds `sale`, `sale_void` and `return` at the
    branch within the last N days. A suggested PO covers the supplier's preferred items, or all its
    items when it is preferred for none; the line's price is the last price × the purchase unit's
    factor. The low-stock list is every variant with a rule at or below min (on hand + on order)
    and every negative balance.
23. **Due date and POs.** A credit receipt without a due date is due on the invoice date + the
    supplier's terms; a cash receipt on the invoice date. A PO closes by itself once every line has
    received at least its quantity; voiding a receipt reopens a PO that had closed by itself, while
    one closed by hand stays closed.
24. **Field lengths.** Supplier code 1–20 characters (trimmed, upper case, no spaces), supplier
    item code at most 40, invoice number 1–60, batch at most 40, notes at most 500.
